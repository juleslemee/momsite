Melissa Zexter

Interview and Studio Visit with the Textile Arts Center

July 31, 2013

Melissa Zexter is a Highlighted Artist with the Textile Arts Center. The studio visit and interview gives much insight into Zexter's process and inspiration, as she speaks on her relationship with embroidery and photographic work, on a conceptual and physical level. Zexter also discusses where she is heading with her work, as she continues to experiment within the world of thread and photography.
