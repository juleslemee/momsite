Shop Muriel Guepin Gallery on 1stDibs

The Muriel Guépin Gallery features artists who embrace intricate, labor-intensive techniques, emphasizing material, craftsmanship and dedication. A core focus is nature-inspired art, with many artists exploring landscapes, organic forms and patterns found in nature. Through diverse medium such as delicate drawings, paintings, manipulated photographs, sculptures, ceramic and textiles, the gallery showcases works that reflect both the beauty and complexity of the natural world.
