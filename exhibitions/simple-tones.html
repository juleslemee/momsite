Simple Tones

Exploring black and white

The austerity of black and white art begs to search the soul and offer plain and simple truths.

July 3 – August 29, 2020


Why is black and white wall art so appealing? 
Taking away the colors takes away the distractions, allowing us to see subjects in a very different way. The austerity of black and white art begs to search the soul and offer plain and simple truths.
