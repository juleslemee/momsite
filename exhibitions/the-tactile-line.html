The Tactile Line

How Inni Parnannen, Mane Phély and Erin Vicent translate the quiet geometries of nature into intimate structures

September 12 – November 2, 2025

The Tactile Line explores how three artists translate the quiet geometries of nature into intimate structures. Mane Phely draws from the rugged rhythms and fissures of tree bark, while Inni Pärnänen and Erin Vincent’s toothpick series echo floral symmetries—radial blooms, spirals, and seedhead grids—into airy lattices. Together, bark and blossom become structures you can feel, tracing the line where material meets memory.
