One More Week of Summer

Announcing a group show of unique artworks on paper

September 9 – 13, 2015

Because we want summer to last a big longer, we put together a group show with over 75 artworks on paper by a dozen of artists of the gallery. This is a great opportunity to look at plenty of amazing artworks! 

 

The show will be on view until Sunday September 13.
